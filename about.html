About Zovu

Zovu is a social platform built around the places and moments that bring people together — events, universities, and everyday life.

Events

Meet people at events & festivals

When you sign up for an event, swiping opens two weeks before the event starts and closes when the event ends. Therefore, Zovu's event mode connects attendees before and during events.

Event mode is free with a limited number of likes per day. Want more? Upgrade to unlimited likes for that specific event with a one-time purchase.

University

Connect with students at your university

University can be one of the most social periods of your life — but meeting new people isn't always easy. Zovu's university mode lets students connect with others at their own institution, making campus life more social, more fun, and more memorable. University mode is completely free for all students, with a limited number of likes per day. There are no paid upgrades or premium options available for this mode.

Coming soon

Discover people nearby

Zovu's Nearby mode lets you see and connect with other users in your area in real time — no event ticket or student ID required. Whether you're at a café, a park, or just exploring a new city, Nearby makes spontaneous connections possible.
