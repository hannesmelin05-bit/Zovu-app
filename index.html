Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. What We Collect

We collect information you provide when signing up: name, age, gender, email address, and optional profile photo. We also store the events you join and your notification preferences.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate the app, match you with other attendees at events, send notifications you opt into, and improve the service.

3. Who We Share It With

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers necessary to run the app (e.g. Supabase for database hosting). These providers are bound by data processing agreements.

4. Profile Visibility

Your name, age, gender, and photo may be visible to other users who are attending the same events. You can edit or remove this information at any time in your profile settings.

Your data is stored securely on servers within the EU. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.
